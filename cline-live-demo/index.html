What the subscribers say

Questions, answered properly

How fresh is the coffee, really?

Roasted Thursday, posted Friday, usually at your door Monday or Tuesday. We print the roast date on every bag and publish the batch on this page. Nothing in our roastery is older than nine days when it ships.

Can I choose the roast profile?

Yes. Each month has a filter-leaning and an espresso-leaning lot, and Household and Obsession plans can split their bags across both. Solo subscribers pick one and can switch between deliveries.

What if I don't like a bag?

Tell us what was wrong — too dark, stale, wrong grind. We replace it or refund it, no photo of the beans required. In four years we've replaced 1.8% of bags.

How do I pause or cancel?

One click in your account, effective immediately, no email, no phone call, no "are you sure" maze. Your subscription just stops and any paid-but-unsent delivery is refunded.

Do you sell grinders or machines?

No. We roast coffee and we're opinionated about brewing, which is why every delivery includes a recipe card tuned to that exact lot instead of selling you hardware.
